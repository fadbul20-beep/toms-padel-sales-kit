Sponsor's FAQ

The questions every sponsor asks.

Honest answers. If something isn't here, book a call — we'll work through it with you.

What activation opportunities exist beyond a logo on a banner?
+
Everything. Product sampling in the lounge, branded cold-plunge towels, co-branded café drinks with Makna, on-court brand experiences (20-min activation in Gold), winner-board takeovers, IG Reels with our community captains, and bespoke events co-produced with TOMS. Logos on banners are the floor, not the ceiling.
Can we customize the sponsorship package?
+
Yes — the tiers are defaults. If you want court-title sponsorship ("Court Canggu presented by X") for a full year, uniform branding, or a bespoke activation, we build around it. Annual deals typically start Rp 250M and scale to Rp 1B.
Audience demographics — age, gender, profession?
+
Predominantly 25–45, skewing 60/40 male/female, with a concentrated South Jakarta professional base — finance, tech, creative, founders, senior executives. We can share anonymised booking-time and spend patterns under NDA for serious conversations.
How are events promoted leading up to the day?
+
IG-led (@tomspadel) with a 3-week run-up: teaser reels, player spotlights, KV drops, story polls, WhatsApp broadcasts to our 357 verified player list, and cross-posting with Makna Coffee. Day-of, we capture and publish an after-movie within 72 hours.
What's the venue occupancy rate?
+
Overall YTD 38.2%. Normal (non-Ramadhan) periods run 49.0% average with 100% peak days recorded. Peak hour (20:00) sits at 70% post-expansion to 4 courts. Weekend saturation (Sat+Sun) accounts for 43.7% of all bookings.
What's the target audience per event, by number?
+
A standard TOMS-produced tournament runs ~60–80 active players (16 teams × 4–5 players) with 150–220 total on-site including spectators and partners. Eyeview contact over a 2-day event lands in the 1,500–2,500 range, with digital reach across @tomspadel and co-sponsors adding 15–40k impressions.
